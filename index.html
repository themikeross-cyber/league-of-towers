League of Towers

Place champions along a single lane, then hold the nexus for five rounds. Towers attack on their own. Waves are short, so a full run can finish in under two minutes.

Press 1–5 to pick a champion, click the lane to place them, then hit Start Round. Right-click a champion between rounds to sell them for a full refund.
